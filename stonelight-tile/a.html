A legacy of ceramic artistry since 1920.

We continue the tradition of S&S Tile, using original authentic 1920s molds, plates, and presses to produce classic tiles. Our work suits Arts and Crafts, Mission Revival, Mediterranean, Spanish Colonial, Victorian, Edwardian, and Art Deco styles.

Our design team helps you mix and match low cost imported tile, stone or glass with our handmade products to achieve a full custom look.
